## New

- Rounds. Watch a course again from the start: Course menu, then Start a new round. Every round before is kept, with how far you got and when. Each lesson shows how many times you watched it. When you finish a course, a button offers the next round.
- The course note in your vault keeps the round, the rounds before, and how many times you watched each lesson. Another phone on the same vault follows a new round.
- Set up a course when you add it. ObsiLearn shows what it found and lets you pick: one course or many, how folders become modules, and if PDFs and slides belong to a video or are lessons of their own. You can rename the course. Every choice shows the result at once.
- Change it later: Course menu, then Change how it is shown. Your progress and highlights stay.
