# ObsiLearn 0.4.4

- **Much better transcripts on the phone.** The old phone model got many words wrong. It is replaced by two far more exact ones, picked for your phone's memory: Parakeet (670 MB, English and 24 other European languages) or Moonshine (290 MB, English). They are free, work offline, and the sound never leaves the phone. The model is fetched once, the first time you make a transcript. Use Wi-Fi.
- **Clean up with AI.** A new setting in Settings, Courses and transcripts. After a transcript is made, your text model fixes misheard words, stops and capitals. Only the text is sent, not the sound, so it costs few tokens. Each word keeps its time, so highlights still land on the right word. If the AI is off or fails, you keep the transcript as it was.
- Old phone transcripts do not change by themselves. To redo one, open the lesson's menu and tap "Make the transcript again".
