# ObsiLearn 0.4.3

- **Highlight right on PDF pages.** Hold a word on the page, then drag to the last word. The highlight snaps to whole words. Tap a highlight to add a note, tags or a colour. It shows in the text view too, and goes to review, search and Obsidian like every other highlight. Needs Android 15 or newer.
- **Much more exact transcripts.** When a speech service is set up, transcripts now use it by itself. Before, they stayed on the small phone model, which gets many words wrong. You can still pick "On this phone" in Settings, Courses and transcripts.
- **Cleaner sound for the service.** High hiss is taken out before the sound is sent, pieces are cut at a quiet moment so no word is cut in two, and the lesson title is sent along so names are spelled right.
- **Transcripts keep their capitals, commas and full stops.**
- **Groq uses its most exact model**, Whisper large v3.
- **OpenRouter makes transcripts.** With an OpenRouter key, you can pick OpenRouter under "Make transcripts with".
