# ObsiLearn 0.2.0

## Files

- **Files row.** Each lesson's PDFs, worksheets and slides show in a row under the video. Tap one to read it.
- **Files tab.** Every file of every course in one place, sorted by kind. Each shows how far you read and its highlights. It takes the place of Up next.
- **Highlight in files.** PDFs, Word files, slides and pages open as text you can highlight, like a transcript. Highlights go to your vault and to Review. Tap the switch at the top to see the original pages.
- **Your place is kept.** A PDF or document opens where you stopped.
- **Smooth PDF pages.** Scrolling no longer jumps. Pinch with two fingers to zoom, or double-tap.
- **Lesson pages** show their pictures and turn dark with the app.
- **Other files** (.doc, .ppt, .rtf, zip and more): the app shows the text it can read, then offers to open the file in another app. A zip shows what is inside.
- **Search inside files.** Search finds words in the files you opened. Course menu › Make files searchable reads all of them at once.
- **Offline** now saves a lesson's files too, and reading lessons.

## Lessons

- **Next lesson.** At the end of a video or a reading lesson, a card takes you to the next lesson, video or reading. Videos go on by themselves (Settings › Go on to the next lesson).
- Each lesson now shows its course picture, not a blank box.
- Home shows "Reading · 40% in" or "Stopped at 12:30", not "Almost done" for everything.
- Course and folder names no longer show web addresses like www.udemy.com.
- Course notes in your vault count the highlights in a lesson's files too.

## Help when you are stuck

- **NAS address:** spaces written as %20, a missing http://, or a Windows path like `\\nas\share` now work. When WebDAV is off on the NAS, the app says so and tries the plain network share.
- **Google Drive:** each problem says what to do, not a code.

## In every Obsi app

- **One look for all apps.** Change the colour, light or dark, or the font in one app. The other apps change too when you open them. A look you picked before this update is carried over.
- **Purple is the first look.** A new app starts in the Obsidian purple, until you pick another.
- **Your stats.** A card on Home shows your streak, time and finished items this week, for every Obsi app. Tap it for the full page.
- **New app icons.** No white edge any more. Settings › Look shows the real icon in five colours: amethyst, ruby, sapphire, emerald and amber.
- **Plain zeros.** The number 0 no longer has a slash through it.
- **Clearer errors.** No internet, a slow server or a wrong address now says what to do, not a code.
- Each app still works alone. The sharing only adds to it.
