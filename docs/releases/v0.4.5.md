# ObsiLearn 0.4.5

- **A cleaner lesson menu.** The menu now shows a few quick buttons and short groups. Tap a group to open it: Transcript, Offline and Files each have their own page, so you only see the controls you want.
- **Do more with a transcript, any time.** In the Transcript page you can refine the transcript with AI, or make it again on the phone or with your key.
- **Pick your quick buttons.** In "Quick buttons", tick up to 4 actions to show at the top of the menu.
- **PDFs show every picture.** The reading view now finds each picture where the PDF puts it. Pieces of text no longer show up as pictures. Logos on every page and tiny marks are left out. Pictures that overlap show as one.
- **Offline lessons keep their transcripts.** Saving a lesson for offline now also saves its subtitle file or podcast transcript. Lessons you saved before get theirs the next time the app opens with a network.
