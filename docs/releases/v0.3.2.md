# ObsiLearn 0.3.2

- **Your place in an episode is safe.** In 0.3.1, a feed refresh that came back short could take episodes out of the library, and their place with them. Now only Unfollow or Remove takes episodes out. Episodes that drop off a feed stay, with their place and done mark. A feed that comes back empty is ignored.
- **The media card works after Android closes the app.** Play on the notification, the lock screen or headphones now starts the last episode again, at its saved place.
- **The queue starts each episode where you stopped it**, not at the start.
