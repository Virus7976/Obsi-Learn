# ObsiLearn 0.3.1

## Across every Obsi app

- **Search finds what you mean.** Search no longer matches letters spread over a long title. Before, "lonly" found "**Lon**g-Distance Re**l**ationships … Manduley", so one big podcast filled every search. Typos close together still work: "lonly" finds "Lonely", and word starts still work: "ldr" finds "Long-Distance Relationships".

## ObsiLearn

- **Unfollow really removes a show.** When you unfollow a podcast or remove a course, its episodes and lessons leave the library. They no longer show in search or in "Pick up where you left off". An episode with your highlights, tags, a pin or a playlist stays, so nothing you made is lost.
- **Made transcripts stay in time with the sound.** Some podcasts put in different ads each time the episode downloads. Then a transcript made from one download drifts against the copy you hear. Now ObsiLearn saves the episode first, makes the transcript from that copy, and plays that same copy. If the episode is playing, it moves to the saved copy at the same second. For an older made transcript that is out of time, tap More › Make the transcript again.
