# ObsiLearn 0.6.1

- **Cleaner PDFs.** In a PDF's text view, lines printed on page after page are left out: a copyright note, a title at the top, page numbers. The "Page 10" labels are gone too. Listen skips them, so you hear only the real text.
- To see them again, tap ⋮ in the PDF, then Hide repeated page text.
- A highlight you made before stays on its words.
