## New

- Lesson files open inside ObsiLearn: PDFs, Word files, slides, sheets, lesson pages, Markdown, text and pictures. Another app is only one tap away, at the top right.
- A lesson that is only reading opens straight to its file, with a Mark as done button.
- The player menu lists the lesson's files.
- Save a lesson for offline, or as audio only, from the player menu.

Slides show their text, pictures and speaker notes, not their exact layout.
