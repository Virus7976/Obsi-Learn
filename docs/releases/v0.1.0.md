# ObsiLearn 0.1.0

The first version. Watch your video courses from wherever they are, and keep what you learn in Obsidian.

- Add a course from a folder on the phone, an SD card or USB stick, Google Drive, WebDAV, a Windows or NAS share (SMB), your PC hub, a web folder, a podcast feed, or a list of links.
- Modules and lessons are found from the folders and file numbers. Subtitles, pages and PDFs go with their lesson.
- Save a lesson, a module or a whole course for offline, as video or as sound only.
- Make transcripts for lessons that have none: on the phone (free, offline) or with your own key (fast). For one lesson, a module or a whole course.
- Highlight the transcript and save it to Obsidian, the same as in ObsiTube.
- One note per course in your vault keeps your progress. Tick a lesson in Obsidian and the app sees it. Your own text under the line stays. Settings, then Vault notes, shows each note and lets you keep them out of search.
- Part of the Obsi apps: switch to ObsiTube, ObsiRead and Obsi Bible from Settings, and share one review.
